The questions we get every week

Quick answers to slow-killing SEO problems.

Q.Why isn't my new page ranking?

Nine times out of ten it's an internal linking problem, not a content problem. We check crawl depth first.

Q.Do I need to publish daily?

No — one well-targeted page a week outperforms daily filler. Volume never beat relevance.

Q.Is my site being penalized?

Usually not "penalized" — just outranked. We'll show you the gap in a 15-minute audit call.

Q.How fast can rankings actually move?

Technical fixes: days. Content and authority: 6–12 weeks. Anyone promising faster is guessing.

Q.Should I switch to AI-written content?

Only if it's edited by someone who knows the topic. Unedited AI content is exactly what's getting filtered.

Q.What's the one thing to fix first?

Whatever's costing you the most traffic — that's what a real audit tells you, not a generic checklist.
